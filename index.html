How I take a board to production, as a scroll

Design

I design the board and the firmware together

Schematic capture, multi-layer layout and power-rail planning happen alongside the firmware architecture, so neither side surprises the other.

Reviews catch problems before fabrication

I lead hardware, schematic and PCB design reviews, chasing signal-integrity and power issues before they reach production. At Zennovation Labs, 9 of 10 board revisions worked first pass.

Bring-up is a process, not a scramble

Custom drivers and device tree overlays cut bring-up time for new board revisions from 10 days to 2 days at Melior ITS.

Then tune for the battery

Sleep-cycle and duty-cycle tuning of Wi-Fi, BLE and MQTT stacks extended battery life by 48% on low-power wearables.

About

I’m an embedded systems engineer with three-plus years of building production firmware for IoT, industrial and home-automation devices, from bare-metal STM32 drivers to Zephyr-based wireless products.

I like owning the whole path from concept to production: the schematic, the layout, the drivers and the connectivity. That is why this page is built around boards and the things I learned bringing them up.
